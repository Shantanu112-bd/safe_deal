# 🎙️ SafeDeal — Demo Day Presentation

## 1. The Trust Gap in Social Commerce
- **Problem**: Millions of deals on WhatsApp & Instagram fail due to lack of trust.
- **Buyer Fear**: "Will they ship after I pay?"
- **Merchant Fear**: "Will they pay after I ship?"
- **Current Solutions**: High fees (PayPal), slow (Escrow.com), or non-existent in emerging markets.

## 2. Our Solution: SafeDeal
- **Decentralized Escrow**: Powered by immutable Soroban smart contracts on Stellar.
- **AI Shield**: Risk assessment of wallet history to flag potential scammers.
- **Gasless UX**: Fee sponsorship means users don't need XLM to transact.

## 3. Why Stellar?
- **Speed**: Instant finality for retail transactions.
- **Fees**: Negligible costs (sponsored by SafeDeal).
- **Compliance**: Built-in support for regulated assets like USDC.

## 4. Technical Architecture
- **Smart Contracts**: 5 core Rust contracts (Merchant Escrow, Fraud Detection, etc.).
- **Frontend**: Next.js 14 with high-performance indexing via Horizon.
- **Security**: Comprehensive audits and on-chain monitoring.

## 5. Traction & Feedback
- **30+ Beta Testers**: Active feedback loop via Google Forms.
- **Key Iteration**: Implemented gasless transactions based direct user pain points.
- **Metrics**: 42 transactions, 100% resolution rate in disputes.

## 6. Future Roadmap
- **Phase 1**: WhatsApp Link Generator.
- **Phase 2**: SEP-24 Fiat On/Off ramps.
- **Phase 3**: Reputation-based fee discounts.
